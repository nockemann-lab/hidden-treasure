What's hidden inside your phone?

Your phone is a treasure chest of chemistry. Many of its elements are critical raw materials: vital for modern technology, but with supplies at risk.

60–70chemical elements can be found in a smartphone
93%of the metal value comes from gold, silver, platinum and palladium, which make up under 1.5% of the metal
22%of the world's e-waste was properly recycled in 2022

📱 What Can YOU Do?

♻️ Recycle Your Old Phones Take them to a certified e-waste collection point. Never put phones or batteries in the household bin, because damaged lithium-ion batteries can start fires.
🔧 Repair, Don't Replace Keep phones longer by repairing instead of buying new.
🤝 Donate or Resell Working phones can have a second life with someone else.
📚 Spread Awareness Tell others about the value hidden in electronic waste!

The 5 billion phones expected to be thrown away in 2022 contained metals worth an estimated £6.7 billion (US$9.25 billion). We only get that value back if phones are collected and recycled. 🌍

Yet in 2022 only 22% of the world's electronic waste was documented as properly collected and recycled.

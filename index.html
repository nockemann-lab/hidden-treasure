A smartphone can contain 60 to 70 different chemical elements – about 30 of them are used in most phones.

💡 Did you know? Copper is more than half of all the metal in a phone. But gold, silver, platinum and palladium – less than 1.5% of the metal – make up 93% of its value!

📱 What Can YOU Do?

♻️ Recycle Your Old Phones Take them to a certified e-waste collection point. Never put phones or batteries in the household bin – damaged lithium-ion batteries can start fires.
🔧 Repair, Don't Replace Keep phones longer by repairing instead of buying new.
🤝 Donate or Resell Working phones can have a second life with someone else.
📚 Spread Awareness Tell others about the value hidden in electronic waste!

The 5 billion phones expected to be thrown away in 2022 contained metals worth an estimated US$9.25 billion – value we only get back if phones are collected and recycled. 🌍

Yet in 2022 only 22% of the world's electronic waste was documented as properly collected and recycled.
